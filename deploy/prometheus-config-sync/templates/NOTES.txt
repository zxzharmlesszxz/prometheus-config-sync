prometheus-config-sync is installed as a single writer.

Generated files are written to PVC {{ include "prometheus-config-sync.claimName" . }} at
{{ .Values.config.outputDir }}. Mount the same claim read-only into Prometheus and configure:

scrape_config_files:
  - {{ .Values.config.outputDir }}/scrape-configs*.yml
rule_files:
  - {{ .Values.config.outputDir }}/rules/*.yml

Preserve {{ .Values.config.outputDir }}/.prometheus-config-sync-applied.sha256
across config-sync restarts. It records the generation successfully reloaded by Prometheus.
